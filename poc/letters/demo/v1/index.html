How to read a letter

Each letter is drawn three ways at once, from the brightest point outwards.

Thirty-nine palettes

Each sound set owns a band of the colour wheel and wears a coloured ring, stepping darker to lighter through the set in table order (բ պ փ). In Western the first and the last letter of a set sound alike; in Eastern they are voiced, plain and aspirated. The other Mashtots letters take the hues left between the sets, each as far as it can get from the hues already used; yellows and greens are lifted, because they are only beautiful light. The two later arrivals are moonlight, and the ligature is copper. Click a letter to fly to it.
