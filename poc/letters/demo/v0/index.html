Letter Constellation

Each star is an Armenian letter. A small language model read four gigabytes of Armenian and learned a vector for every letter; letters it treats alike sit close together here. Nobody told it how the letters sound.

How to read it

Drag to turn the sky; click a letter to read its card. Threads join each letter to its two nearest letters in the model. A coloured thread means both ends belong to the same set of three.

Gold letters are Mashtots' 36. Silver Օ and Ֆ arrived around the 12th century. Copper և is the ligature of ե and ւ.

    What is next

    A letters-only model, trained once on Eastern and once on Western text, will give every symbol a learned place, including Օ, Ֆ and և. The sky will then move between the two dialects. The Western half grows as the diaspora's texts are added.
